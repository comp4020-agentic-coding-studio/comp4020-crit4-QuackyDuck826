# Process overview

## What I built

I made an loop based instrument inspired by incredibox, where you play freely with which sections/beats play while they all remain in time and in tune in a way that makes it impossible to play wrong.

## The moments that mattered

When peiceing differnt simple melodies together claude was able to make things sound cohesive very quickly, keeping a constant time signature and pitch range, but seemed to have very little awareness of how the loudness of differnt pitches feels through speakers. while the sounds all still had individual labels I prompted to make specific ones louder or quieter. For some of the bass sounds it wasnt enough to simply boost the sound, and instead I asked that the pitch be changed slightly to be closer to the louder part of the range. Now all sounds can be heard distinctly when you play any select 5, and when everything is on there isnt anything that feels too overpowering as it had before.
